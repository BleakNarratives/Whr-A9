## **Ad Copy Best Practices**

### **Headlines:**
- Include main keyword
- Highlight unique value proposition
- Use action-oriented language
- Keep under 30 characters

### **Descriptions:**
- Focus on benefits, not features
- Include a clear call-to-action
- Use numbers and statistics
- Keep under 90 characters

### **Extensions:**
- Use all available extensions
- Highlight key differentiators
- Include social proof
- Link to relevant pages

### **Landing Pages:**
- Match ad copy to page content
- Clear call-to-action above fold
- Mobile-optimized design
- Fast load times

## **Bidding Strategy**

- **Start with:** Manual CPC bidding
- **Target CPA:** $50-$100 (adjust based on conversion value)
- **Budget:** $500-$1,000/day per campaign
- **Optimize for:** Conversions (demo requests)
- **Adjust based on:** Conversion rate, CPA, ROI

---

## **A/B Testing Plan**

Test these variations:
1. **Headlines:** "Evolve Processes" vs "Optimize Workflows"
2. **Descriptions:** Benefit-focused vs feature-focused
3. **CTAs:** "Try Free" vs "Get Demo" vs "Learn More"
4. **Extensions:** Different sitelink combinations
5. **Landing Pages:** Homepage vs dedicated demo page

---

## **Performance Metrics**

Track these KPIs:
- **CTR:** > 3% (aim for 5%+)
- **Conversion Rate:** > 5% (aim for 10%+)
- **CPA:** < $100 (adjust based on LTV)
- **ROAS:** > 3x (aim for 5x+)
- **Quality Score:** 7+ (aim for 9-10)
